Rawlight — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 2026 · Applies to the Rawlight Android app (com.rawlight.app).

TL;DR: Rawlight has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your photos never leave your device unless you share them.

What we collect

Nothing. Rawlight transmits no data anywhere. There is no server, no account, no crash-reporting service, no advertising SDK.

Data stored on your device

  • Photos you take — saved to your device's public Pictures/Rawlight folder via Android's MediaStore, exactly as any camera app does.
  • Device camera profile — technical capabilities of your phone's camera hardware, stored in the app's private storage to tune image quality.
  • Your preferences — settings such as aspect ratio and watermark text, stored privately on-device.

Permissions

  • Camera — required to take photos. This is the app.
  • Approximate location (optional) — Android's coarse location, requested only if you switch on GPS tagging in Settings → EXIF. The coordinates go into your photo's EXIF tag; they are never sent anywhere by the app. Off by default.
  • Internet — used solely by Google Play to process the optional one-time Pro upgrade. The app itself makes no network requests.
  • Foreground service (camera) — while an intervalometer or bracket sequence runs, Android requires a visible notification and a short-lived wake lock to keep the run alive screen-off. No data is collected or sent; everything ends when the sequence stops.

In-app purchase

The optional one-time Pro upgrade is processed entirely by Google Play. Rawlight never sees your payment details.

Children

Rawlight contains no content or features directed at children and no data collection of any kind, from anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the Play Store support email listed on the app's store page.
